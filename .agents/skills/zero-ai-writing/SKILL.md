# Zero-AI Writing Skill (Strict Anti-AI Prose Rules)

> **MANDATORY DIRECTIVE:** Every piece of prose, article, documentation, report, or text generated or edited under this skill MUST strictly obey all rules below. Violating even ONE rule invalidates the output and requires an immediate rewrite before presenting to the user.

---

## 1. Punctuation & Formatting Prohibitions

1. **Absolute Ban on Em-Dashes (`—`) and En-Dashes (`–`) in Prose:**
   - NEVER use `—` or ` - ` to interrupt a sentence, insert parenthetical explanations, or deliver a dramatic punchline at the end of a clause.
   - **Required replacement:** Split into two separate sentences with a period (`.`), use a standard comma (`,`), or use parentheses `(...)` strictly for brief factual data.
2. **Ban on Formulaic Bold-Colon Bullet Lists (`- **Heading:** Explanation`):**
   - Do not format lists where every bullet starts with a bolded noun phrase followed by a colon or dash.
   - Prefer continuous, well-structured paragraphs. Use bullet points only for raw data, steps, or genuine lists without repetitive bold prefixes.
3. **Ban on Decorative Mid-Sentence Boldface:**
   - Do not randomly bold keywords or phrases inside paragraphs for emphasis unless explicitly requested by the user.

---

## 2. Banned Formulaic Transitions & Connectors

**NEVER start a sentence or paragraph with these mechanical connectors:**

* **Vietnamese:**
  * *Hơn nữa, / Thêm vào đó, / Ngoài ra, / Bên cạnh đó,*
  * *Đáng chú ý là, / Điều đáng chú ý là, / Cần lưu ý rằng, / Điều quan trọng cần nhớ là,*
  * *Thật vậy, / Quả thực, / Rõ ràng là, / Không thể phủ nhận rằng,*
  * *Trong bối cảnh... / Trong kỷ nguyên... / Trong lĩnh vực... / Khi nói đến... / Về cốt lõi,*
  * *Tóm lại, / Nhìn chung, / Suy cho cùng, / Cuối cùng nhưng không kém phần quan trọng, / Hướng tới tương lai,*
* **English:**
  * *Furthermore, / Moreover, / In addition, / Additionally, / Consequently, / Nonetheless, / Nevertheless,*
  * *Crucially, / Importantly, / Notably, / Specifically, / Indeed, / Undoubtedly,*
  * *It is worth noting that / It is important to remember that / In the realm of / In the context of / When it comes to / At its core,*
  * *In conclusion, / To summarize, / Ultimately, / Overall, / All in all, / Looking ahead, / Moving forward,*

**Required replacement:** Connect sentences through direct cause-and-effect or subject continuity. If sentence B logically follows sentence A, place it directly after sentence A with zero transitional filler.

## 3. Banned Robotic Syntactic Templates

1. **No Negative Parallelisms:**
   - **BANNED:** *"Không chỉ là X, mà còn là Y"*, *"Không đơn thuần là X, mà là Y"*, *"Đây không phải là X, mà là Y"* / *"It is not just X, it is Y"*, *"Not merely X, but rather Y"*.
   - **Rule:** State directly what the subject is or does without contrasting it against what it is not.
2. **No Trailing `-ing` Participial Clauses of Significance:**
   - **BANNED:** Ending a factual sentence with a comma followed by an abstract commentary clause:
     - Vietnamese: *"...qua đó khẳng định..."*, *"...góp phần thúc đẩy..."*, *"...làm nổi bật tầm quan trọng của..."*, *"...đánh dấu một bước ngoặt..."*, *"...mở đường cho..."*, *"...là minh chứng cho..."*.
     - English: *"...highlighting...", "...underscoring...", "...showcasing...", "...fostering...", "...solidifying...", "...reflecting...", "...serving as a testament to..."*.
   - **Rule:** End the sentence with a period immediately after stating the concrete fact. Let the facts speak for themselves.
3. **No Compulsive Rule of Three:**
   - Do not group adjectives, nouns, or verbs into rhythmic triads (e.g., *"nhanh chóng, hiệu quả và toàn diện"* / *"clear, concise, and compelling"*). Pick the single most accurate word.
4. **No Fake Concession Openers (`Mặc dù X, nhưng Y` / `While X, Y`):**
   - Do not open paragraphs or concluding sentences with *"While [obvious statement], [actual point]..."* or *"Mặc dù..., nhưng..."*. State the main point immediately.
5. **No False-Range Sweepers (`Từ X đến Y` / `From X to Y`):**
   - Do not use phrases like *"Từ người mới bắt đầu đến chuyên gia..."* or *"From small startups to global enterprises..."*. Specify the exact target group.

## 4. Banned AI Puffery & Cliché Vocabulary

**NEVER use the following inflated words and metaphors:**

* **Vietnamese:** *Bức tranh toàn cảnh, bức tranh đa sắc màu, ngọn hải đăng, minh chứng sống động, cột mốc lịch sử, bước ngoặt, nền tảng cốt lõi, không ngừng biến đổi, đa chiều, toàn diện, sâu sắc, liền mạch, tối ưu hóa (khi dùng chung chung không có số liệu), nâng tầm, khai phá, thúc đẩy, định hình tương lai, kỷ nguyên mới.*
* **English:** *Delve, tapestry, testament, beacon, crucial, vital, pivotal, multifaceted, intricate, nuanced, foster, showcase, underscore, realm, landscape (figurative), seamless, leverage, game-changer, groundbreaking, unwavering, symphony, interplay, cornerstone, catalyst, paradigm.*

---

## 5. Required Human Rhythm & Verification Checklist

Before outputting any text, run this mandatory self-verification pass:

1. **Check for `—` or `–`:** Search the draft for any em-dash or en-dash. If found, replace with a period or comma.
2. **Check Sentence Openings:** Scan the first 3 words of every sentence. Delete any transitional adverb or throat-clearing phrase.
3. **Check Sentence Endings:** Scan the last clause of every sentence. Delete any summary commentary about "significance", "impact", or "legacy".
4. **Vary Sentence Cadence:** Mix longer analytical sentences (20-30 words) with short, blunt sentences (3-8 words). Never write 3 consecutive sentences of similar length.
5. **Replace Adjectives with Data:** Replace vague claims (*"cải thiện đáng kể"*, *"hiệu suất vượt trội"*) with specific numbers, mechanisms, or concrete actions.
6. **No Throat-Clearing Introductions or Summary Conclusions:** Start with the core information in sentence one. Stop immediately when the last fact is delivered.
